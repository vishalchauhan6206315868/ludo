# Privacy Policy for Ludo Supreme

**Last updated:** October 01, 2026

**Allcore Solutions** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Ludo Supreme** mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### A. Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data
While using our Service, especially when using Google Sign-In or creating a player profile, we may collect certain personally identifiable information:
- **Display Name / Full Name**: To identify you within the game, display on match screens, and show on leaderboards.
- **Email Address**: Used solely as your account identifier for secure authentication and account recovery via Firebase Authentication.
- **Profile Picture URL**: To display your profile photo during gameplay.

#### Gameplay & App Data
- **Game Progress & Scores**: Information related to your game statistics, wins, losses, coins, and match records.
- **Preferences**: Sound effects, music volume, and visual preferences stored locally on your device.

#### Log Data & Diagnostics
When you use our Service, we may collect information about crashes and diagnostics through Firebase Analytics:
- Device model and manufacturer
- Operating system version
- Anonymized unique device identifiers
- App crash reports and performance metrics

### B. What We DO NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect or request access to your Contacts, SMS, Phone Call logs, Camera, Microphone, or precise GPS location.
- We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

---

## 2. Use of Data

Allcore Solutions uses the collected data for the following purposes:
- To provide, operate, and maintain **Ludo Supreme**
- To facilitate matchmaking between players
- To authenticate your identity securely via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth
- To save your game progress, scores, and preferences
- To monitor the usage of the Service and detect, prevent, and address technical issues and crashes
- To provide customer support

## 4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. All data transmitted between the app and our backend/Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). While no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Service is intended for a general audience and does not address anyone under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us at **allcoresolution@gmail.com**. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take immediate steps to remove that information from our servers.

## 6. User Rights and Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policies, users have the right to request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

### How to Request Account and Data Deletion:
1. Send an email to **allcoresolution@gmail.com** with the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request - Ludo Supreme"**.
2. Mention your registered email address and display name in the email body.
3. Upon receipt and verification of your request, we will permanently delete your user account, authentication profile, and associated gameplay records from our database within **7 business days**.
