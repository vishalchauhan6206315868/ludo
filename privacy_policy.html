Privacy Policy

Ludo Supreme • Published by Allcore Solutions

Effective Date: October 01, 2026

Allcore Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ludo Supreme mobile application (the "App", "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (via Google Sign-In)

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect basic profile details:

  • Name: To identify you in the game, on leaderboards, and during matches.
  • Email Address: Used solely for secure authentication and account identity via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed to other players during matches.

B. Gameplay & Progress Data

  • Game statistics, match results, coins balance, and achievements.
  • Sound, music, and in-game preferences stored locally on your device.

C. Diagnostics & Performance Data

We use Firebase Analytics to monitor app stability and fix technical glitches:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Anonymized crash logs and performance indicators.
What We DO NOT Collect: We never request access to your Contacts, Camera, Microphone, SMS, Call Logs, or precise GPS location. We never sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or brokers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the core gameplay features of Ludo Supreme.
  • To authenticate your player account securely.
  • To facilitate multiplayer matchmaking with other users.
  • To save your coins, scores, and preferences across sessions.
  • To detect and fix bugs, errors, and application crashes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted Google services to power core app features. These third parties have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system integration (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In: Player identity & authentication (Identity Policies)
  • Firebase Authentication & Analytics (Google LLC): Account management and crash analytics (Firebase Privacy)

4. Data Security

The security of your personal data is paramount. All data transmitted between the app and Firebase cloud infrastructure is encrypted using modern industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our Service is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at allcoresolution@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such records.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Protection policies, you have full ownership of your data and can request complete account deletion at any time.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:

To permanently delete your player account and all associated personal data, send an email from your registered account to allcoresolution@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Ludo Supreme". Your account, authentication profile, and gameplay data will be permanently purged within 7 business days.
